Employee Performance Dashboard (Excel)
​An interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze employee performance, compensation, and work modes across departments and regions.
​**Key Highlights:**
​Salary & Bonus Analysis: Department-wise breakdown of compensation.
​Performance Tracking: Employee scores and regional distribution.
​Work Mode Insights: Project share and training hours across Hybrid, Remote, and Office setups.
​Tools & Features Used:
​Microsoft Excel
​Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
​Interactive Slicers (Region & Department)
​Data Formatting & Dashboard Design
